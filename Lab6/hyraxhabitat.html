The Hyrax's Habitat

Almost all hyraxes are limited to Africa; the exception is the rock hyrax which is also found in adjacent parts of the Middle East.

Hyraxes are very adaptable. In East Africa, they are found at sea level and at altitudes of more than 4,000 meters (14,000 feet).
Their habitats range from dry savanna to dense rainforest to cold Afro-alpine moorland.
