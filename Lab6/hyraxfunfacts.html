Hyrax Fun Facts

  • Infant hyraxes are born so fully developed they can run and jump an hour after birth! Although suckled until they are 3-months-old, the young begin to eat vegetation by their second day.
  • They're potty trained! Hyraxes regularly use “latrines,” in the areas they inhabit, conspicuous white deposits from their urine form on rock faces.
  • Male rock hyraxes sing long, complex territorial songs that use syntax; meaning the order of their vocal syllables matters, and feature distinct local dialects depending on the region.
