Twice

Members Albums Achievments

Twice has won over 100 major music awards and more than 120 music show trophies, totaling over 220 wins throughout their career. A few notable awards won from outside of Korea include the Breakthrough Award from Billboard Women in Music, Top 20 Albums of the Year from Asian Pop Music Awards, and a nomination for Favorite K-Pop Artist from the American Music Awards.

Twice has topped the charts of South Korean music programs 97 times, as of their promotion for Feel Special. They also currently rank second as the girl group with most music program wins, behind Girls Generation with 100 wins.
